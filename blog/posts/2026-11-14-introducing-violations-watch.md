Violations Watch is an open log of reported violations of international humanitarian and human rights law, collected from news and social media and shared as leads for investigators, journalists and researchers.

## What it is

Every entry records what was reported, by whom and when, with links to the original reports and, where possible, archived copies. Reports of the same event are merged into one incident with a permanent number, such as VW-2026-0041, so later reports and corrections attach to the same record.

## What it isn't

Most entries are **unverified leads, not findings**. A lead says that something was reported and is worth checking. When an analyst has reviewed or independently verified an incident, its label changes.

## How to use it

- Filter the log by country, priority, violation type or alleged perpetrator.
- Download the full data as CSV or JSON.
- Cite incidents by their VW number.

If something is wrong, email violationswatch@proton.me with the incident number and a source.
