04

For UN monitors and investigators

These leads come from news outlets, UN bodies, rights groups and local media, read every day in eight languages. Each is checked for when and where it happened and how it is sourced, and claims from parties to a conflict are flagged. They have not been verified on the ground: the UN verifies cases through its Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism.

Please cite incidents by VW number, for example VW-2026-0001. Each has its own page with the sources and archived copies. For corrections or questions, write to violationswatch@proton.me. Where else to send information.
