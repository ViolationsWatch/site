Accountability bodies

Where a lead can go next. Violations Watch is an early collection point: it records what has been reported. Verifying, investigating and pursuing accountability is the work of the bodies below. This page lists the UN mandates, investigative mechanisms and regional human rights systems that cover the countries in the log, and how to send them information.

Before you submit anything: get the informed consent of victims and witnesses, never share identifying details over insecure channels, and use each body's own secure contact route. Mandates are renewed, changed or ended regularly, so check the body's own page for its current status.
01

Global mechanisms

UN Special Representative for Children and Armed Conflict

Leads the UN's work on the six grave violations against children. Country task forces verify cases through the Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism set up under Security Council resolution 1612.

Send information The mechanism works through UN country task forces (usually UNICEF and the UN mission or Resident Coordinator in the country concerned), not a public form.

UN Special Procedures (Special Rapporteurs and Working Groups)

Independent experts with thematic mandates (for example on extrajudicial executions, torture, enforced disappearances, internally displaced persons and violence against women) and country mandates. They can raise individual cases with governments. See what a communication is.

Send information Online submission form. Anyone can submit; the consent of the victim or family is normally needed.

Human Rights Council investigative bodies

Commissions of inquiry, fact-finding missions and investigative mechanisms created for specific countries. OHCHR keeps the full list; the ones covering countries in this log are set out below.

Send information Each body publishes its own call for submissions and secure contact details.

International Criminal Court: Office of the Prosecutor

Investigates and prosecutes genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes where the Court has jurisdiction. See the situations under investigation.

Send information Contact the Office of the Prosecutor, including its OTPLink evidence submission platform.

UN treaty bodies: individual complaints

Committees that monitor the human rights treaties (including the Committee on the Rights of the Child, the Human Rights Committee and the Committee against Torture) can hear individual complaints against states that have accepted the procedure, usually after domestic remedies are exhausted.

Send information Form and guidance from OHCHR.

Afghanistan

  • Independent Investigative Mechanism for AfghanistanEstablished by the Human Rights Council in October 2025 to collect and preserve evidence of international crimes and serious violations.
  • Special Rapporteur on AfghanistanCountry mandate reporting to the Human Rights Council and General Assembly.

The International Criminal Court also has an open investigation.

DR Congo

  • Independent Commission of Inquiry on North Kivu and South KivuContinues the work of the 2025 OHCHR fact-finding mission on violations in the eastern provinces.
  • Call for submissions (2026)How to send information to the Commission.

The International Criminal Court also has an open investigation.

Haiti

  • Independent Expert on the situation of human rights in HaitiDesignated expert monitoring and reporting on the situation.

Iran

  • Independent International Fact-Finding Mission on IranInvestigates violations linked to the protests since 2022; mandate extended in January 2026 to cover the protests that began on 28 December 2025.
  • Mandate extension (January 2026)Also extends the Special Rapporteur on Iran.

Myanmar

  • Independent Investigative Mechanism for MyanmarCollects, preserves and analyses evidence of the most serious international crimes since 2011 and shares case files with courts.
  • Special Rapporteur on MyanmarCountry mandate reporting on the human rights situation.

The International Criminal Court has an open investigation into crimes committed partly in Bangladesh.

Palestine (Gaza and West Bank) and Israel

  • Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and IsraelStanding commission investigating violations by all parties since April 2021.
  • Special Rapporteur on the Palestinian territories occupied since 1967Country mandate reporting to the Human Rights Council and General Assembly.

The International Criminal Court also has an open investigation.

Russia

  • Special Rapporteur on the Russian FederationCountry mandate on the human rights situation inside Russia.

Harm to civilians from the war in Ukraine falls under the Ukraine bodies below.

Somalia

  • Independent Expert on SomaliaCountry mandate providing monitoring and technical advice.

South Sudan

  • Commission on Human Rights in South SudanInvestigates violations and collects evidence with a view to accountability.

Sudan

  • Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the SudanInvestigates violations and abuses in the conflict that began in April 2023.
  • Call for submissionsHow to send information to the Mission.
  • African Commission joint fact-finding mission on SudanAfrican Union and African Commission mission; its call for documentation of violations.

The International Criminal Court has an open investigation into Darfur.

Syria

  • Commission of Inquiry on the Syrian Arab RepublicInvestigates violations of international law in Syria since March 2011.
  • International, Impartial and Independent Mechanism (IIIM)Builds criminal case files to support prosecutions in national and international courts.
  • Independent Institution on Missing Persons in SyriaWorks to clarify the fate of missing people and support families.

Ukraine

  • Independent International Commission of Inquiry on UkraineInvestigates violations of human rights and humanitarian law in the context of Russia's invasion.
  • UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in UkraineMonitors and publishes regular updates on civilian casualties and violations.
  • OSCE human dimension mechanisms (Moscow Mechanism)Expert missions mandated by OSCE states; several have reported on Ukraine.

The International Criminal Court also has an open investigation.

Countries in the log with no dedicated UN investigative body at the time of checking: Nigeria, Yemen, Ethiopia, Niger, Guinea, India, Pakistan, Thailand, China, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Mexico and Ecuador. For these, the routes are the thematic Special Procedures and treaty bodies above, and the regional systems below. Earlier UN expert bodies on Yemen (ended 2021) and Ethiopia (ended 2023) were not renewed.

03

Regional human rights systems

African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights

Hears complaints ("communications") against African Union states under the African Charter, and can send its own fact-finding missions.

Send information Communications procedure. Individuals and NGOs can submit.

African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child

Monitors the African Children's Charter and hears complaints about violations of children's rights, including in armed conflict.

Send information About communications.

Inter-American Commission on Human Rights

Receives petitions against member states of the Organization of American States and can grant urgent precautionary measures.

Send information How to file a petition (FAQ).

European Court of Human Rights

Hears applications against Council of Europe member states. Russia ceased to be a party in September 2022; the Court can still examine events before that date.

Send information Questions and answers for applicants.

OSCE Office for Democratic Institutions and Human Rights

Supports the Vienna and Moscow Mechanisms, which let OSCE states send expert missions to examine human rights concerns.

There is no individual complaints route.

There is no regional human rights court or commission with a complaints procedure covering Asia or most of the Middle East.
